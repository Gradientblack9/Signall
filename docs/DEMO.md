# Demo walkthrough

The local app opens at http://127.0.0.1:8000. This workspace has actual public-data runs for MeetUniversity, Buffer and Allbirds. A fresh installation starts empty. Record approximately 5-10 minutes of real screen interaction, clearly disclosing that public evaluation answers were used and private questions skipped.

1. Overview: explain the four counts, channel mix, setup rhythm and job activity. Empty counts are intentional before real collection.
2. Company profile: research a public site. Show an excerpt-matched fact beside an assumption. Explain that quote matching is not independent corroboration.
3. Strategy interview: generate a tailored question, save an answer, request a follow-up, skip another question and edit later. Finish with Reassess context.
4. Signal stack: discover sources, inspect score explanations, approve/reject, show a duplicate-domain flag and crawl history. Crawl approved sources.
5. Intelligence feed: inspect actual evidence versus interpretation, factors, dates and reliability. Demonstrate category, competitor, date, relevance, impact and verification filters. If uploading a synthetic note, label it explicitly and keep it separate from actual company evidence; private files require the user's approval for the selected LLM.
6. Monday brief: generate, show ten sections and any explicit coverage shortfall, export MD/HTML/PDF, and add a suggested action.
7. Action tracker: assign an owner/date and move a task to In progress and Completed.
8. Automation & settings: timezone, next scheduled times, manual weekly run, failed/partial logs and monthly audit. Edit the profile, reassess and show the context diff and new ranking.

Suggested timing: setup/research/interview 0:00-2:00; source review/crawl/evidence 2:00-4:00; brief/export/actions 4:00-6:00; company switch/context diff and automation 6:00-8:00. Long operations may be shown from saved genuine job history, with that fact explained. MeetUniversity has three supported executive developments rather than five; do not conceal this shortfall.

`Signal_Stack_Dashboard_Tour.mp4`, when present, is a roughly 48-second screenshot-based UI tour. It does not satisfy the required screen recording. The three actual reports are under `output/assignment-audit/reports/`. The 5-10 minute recording remains a missing deliverable until genuinely recorded.
